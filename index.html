Services Overview

Rapid Electronics Prototyping

Accelerated hardware iteration from schematic capture to functional PCBA. We prioritize speed without compromising Swiss engineering precision.

Embedded Systems

Firmware development for STM32 and ESP32 platforms. Real-time operating systems (RTOS) and bare-metal optimization for industrial applications.

PCB Design

Multi-layer high-speed digital and precision analog layouts. Expertise in EMI/EMC compliance and signal integrity for complex instrumentation.

FPGA & High-Speed Systems

Xilinx Zynq and Artix-7 implementation. High-speed data acquisition (ADC/DAC) and real-time signal processing on programmable logic.

Workflow — Concept to Prototype

01

Concept

Requirement analysis and feasibility studies. Technical specification finalization.

02

Architecture

Component selection and system-level design. Defining communication protocols and power budgets.

03

Design

Schematic capture, PCB layout, and firmware framework development.

04

Build

Sourcing components, assembly, and initial board bring-up in our Geneva lab.

05

Working Prototype

Final verification, technical documentation, and delivery of functional hardware.

Typical turnaround: 4-8 weeks from architecture to functional prototype.
